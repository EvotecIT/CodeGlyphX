QR alignment scoring

On .NET 8 and later, alignment scoring stops after four mismatches in its 25-position pattern because that candidate cannot reach the existing 22/25 acceptance threshold. Accepted scores, candidate order, selected centers and first-best ties remain unchanged. Recognition allowances, format hints, payload oracles and scoring are unchanged.

The diagnostic includes acceptance-boundary patterns, inversion, global and adaptive thresholds, fractional coordinates, transformed and edge patterns, noise and first-perfect ties.
