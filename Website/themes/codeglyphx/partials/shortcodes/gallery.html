How the gallery stays honest

The images, appearance settings and badges are generated from the library at the commit that built this page. Each recipe adds the gallery's output size; logo cards include their sample mark.

  • One source. Appearance settings come from the recipe file the generator compiles. The page adds the output size and save call, and tests compare the published C# and sample marks with their sources.
  • Measured, then published. The badges come from the scan tests. A look that fails one of its {{ scan.checksPerVariant }} reads is fixed or removed, never footnoted.
  • A simulation, not a guarantee. These are software reads of rendered pixels. Print a proof of your final design and scan it on the phones and at the distances you expect.
