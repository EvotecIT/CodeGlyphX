# Qualifying an illustrated QR scene

Keep the editable `.cgxart` recipe, final PNG/SVG/PDF, and software delivery report together. The report's PNG SHA256 identifies the exact PNG checked by the browser. A different image, compression format, viewer, print size or payload needs its own qualification.

1. Record the payload, scene style and seed, physical width, DPI, pixel dimensions and QR scale. Reopen the saved recipe and confirm the payload before sharing a code.
2. Read every software check. A failed original, screen-size or print-raster check needs a design or delivery-size change. **Find a stronger QR** can enlarge the QR or try square data modules; its preview and export recipe show the chosen design. A higher score can still include failures.
3. Open the actual downloaded SVG and PDF in the viewers used for delivery. Confirm the entire QR and four-module quiet zone are visible. SVG artwork and QR contours are vectors; uploaded logos remain images. PDF embeds the final raster artwork.
4. Display the code at its intended size on target screens. Scan with representative Android and iOS devices at expected distances, angles, screen brightness and lighting. Record the device and camera/app version and recovered text. Include an older or lower-spec device where it represents the audience.
5. Print the final PDF at **100% / actual size**. Disable fit-to-page scaling. Measure the artwork width and check the quiet zone. Use the intended paper, printer and print quality. Scan the physical print under normal and less favorable lighting; record any glare, bleed or failed reads.
6. Confirm every successful read returns the exact intended text. A scanner opening a different URL is a failed check. Repeat physical checks after changing size, placement, module shape, palette, logo or print material.
